# FeatureCard

A raised card with a colour-block number, eyebrow, headline, one line of copy and a link — used for the three readiness outcomes and other feature grids.

- **Consumer provides:** a number or short label, an accent (`brand`, `gold` or `teal`), an eyebrow, a headline, one sentence and a link.
- `surface-raised` fill, 1px `line` outline, `radius-md`, `space-6` padding (`space-8` on desktop), `shadow-pop` only for the three home-page outcome cards; in premium sections (RENZOLO OS™ showcase, pricing, Academy, partner pages) the card keeps its outline only, with no shadow and no hover lift.
- The colour block is 48px, `radius-md`, filled with the accent and its matching `on-…` text.
- Eyebrow in `brand-text`, headline in the display family 700, copy in `ink-muted`, link in `teal-text`.
- Lay cards in a responsive grid (min 200px columns, `space-6` gap) on a `surface-tint` band; they stack to one column on phones.
- Keep accents in the fixed order Customers → `brand`, Capital → `gold`, Investors → `teal`.
